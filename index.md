## Course Description

In this course, we will learn research methods and theoretical frameworks necessary to take a more sociotechncical approach to building with machine learning. The earlier part of the course will be focused on methods and philosophy of science/ modeling structures that are shared across disciplines. 
In the later part of the course, students will act as practitioners and researchers of sociotechnical methods, learning them to adapt and apply them in course projects then evaluate and compare them through peer review. Students will write how-to guides on different research methods and work complete projects to address an aspect of that key question each with different methods.

Research methods are essential to knowledge production, and vary widely from discipline to discipline and even across subdisciplines. How researchers discuss their methods also vary: some fields have a standard methods section that requires an introductory text to interpret, other feilds expect a methods section that is standalone, some have relatively implicit methods because the core methods are the defining contours of the feild. High consensus feilds often do not justify methods by make the link between the specific theories and their methods explcit, while low consensus feilds require this exercise. Interdisciplinary work requires untangling these challenges. A sociotechnical lens to understanding AI is exactly that. 


## Spring 2027 Theme

> What is the best way for practitioners to represent their fairness goals for developing an automated tool to help with fair machine learning ? 

We will use this question, operationalized in multiple  ways to apply the different methods that we study and how they interact and work together to build up a more complete answer to this question.  




## Learning outcomes

Upon successful completion of the course students will be able to:
1. Identify research methods from outside of computing that are often applied in social analysis of technical systems
1. Describe and apply the sociotechnical lens to evaluate ML-powered systems
1. Critique and evaluate research methods related to sociotechnical analysis
1. Apply sociotechnical methods to ML system design
1. Develop a critical technical practice
1. Identify and fill in the gaps where methods as described in an article rely on implicit steps due to sub-area norms.

## Evaluation


Students will: 
- prepare for each class session either via reading for small assignments (graded on completion)
- present papers from the literature and lead discussions
- complete a research project applying one or more methods
- contribute to the course sociotechnical methods how to guide
- peer review how to article(s), project proposals, and project papers
- maintain a reflection journal - CTP
- practice applying additional methods through mini projects
- analyze the method and extract it from, relaly find the social science theory 

The grade will be 60% project (including all milestones) and 40% community contributions and in-class activties.  

There will be required preparation for each class session, sometimes this will be a project milestone and other times this work will count towards community contributions. 
Students will frequently present project updates and papers that they have read.
